# GeoGhost Community Rules

_Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Version 1_

The GeoGhost world map is made of pieces that people found on the street and chose to share. These rules keep it a place worth opening. You agree to them before you publish for the first time, and again whenever they change.

**There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.** Posts that break these rules are removed, and people who break them can be blocked from publishing.

## What you can publish

- Stickers, graffiti, murals, posters and tags **you found in public space**, photographed by you.
- Only the cut-out piece is published, with its rough area (about 100 m) and the date. Your original photo, the exact place, the time of day, your device, notes and tags stay on your phone.

## What you can't publish

- Sexual or pornographic content, or anything involving minors in a sexual way.
- Graphic violence, gore, or content that promotes violence, terrorism or self-harm.
- Hate speech or symbols that attack people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or other identity.
- Harassment, threats or content aimed at a specific private person.
- Personal information: faces you can recognise, licence plates, house numbers, names, phone numbers or anything that identifies a private person.
- Advertising, spam, or the same piece posted again and again.
- Anything illegal, or content you don't have the right to share.

When street art itself contains any of the above, don't publish it.

## Reporting and review

- New publishers' first posts are checked before others can see them.
- Anyone can report a post from its page (**··· → Report**), choosing a reason: sexual or violent content, hate or harassment, someone's private information (faces, licence plates, house numbers…), ads or spam, or something else with a short explanation. A post you report disappears for you right away.
- A post reported by three different people is hidden from everyone and goes back into review; its author sees it as *in re-review*. If it is fine, it comes back; if not, it is removed.
- You can block a publisher from any of their posts (**··· → Block**). Their posts disappear for you at once; they are not told. Unblock them in **Settings → Online**.
- Every report is looked at within 24 hours. Posts that break the rules are removed, and authors who break them can be blocked from publishing.

## Your control

- You can retract any post, or all of them, at any time, and delete all your online data in **Settings → Online**. The pieces in your collection stay on your phone.
